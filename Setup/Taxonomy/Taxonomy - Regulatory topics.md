# Regulatory topics (under Topics/)

**This numbered list is the canonical set of topics** — the only valid names for a note's primary topic or `secondary_topics`. Do not invent topics or vary these names; a name that is not on this list is ignored when filing. A topic's `Topics/<name>/` folder is created and populated the first time a qualifying note is filed under it — the orchestrator auto-creates a skeleton `Index.md` the first time a listed topic is used. Brackets indicate the principal instruments and authorities a fintech operating in the UK and EU is most likely to encounter — illustrative, not exhaustive.

1. **AI and Algorithmic Systems** — EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689), AI Liability proposals, EDPB AI guidance; UK pro-innovation approach, FCA/PRA/BoE AI publications; cross-border standards (ISO/IEC 42001 where relevant as commentary).
2. **Payments and E-money** — PSD2, PSD3 and PSR proposals, SEPA Instant Payments Regulation; UK Payment Services Regulations 2017, Electronic Money Regulations 2011, FPS/Faster Payments, PSR (Payment Systems Regulator) directions.
3. **Crypto-assets and Digital Assets** — MiCA (Regulation (EU) 2023/1114), Transfer of Funds Regulation (Travel Rule), DLT Pilot Regime; UK financial promotions regime for cryptoassets, FCA cryptoasset registration under MLRs, HM Treasury phased crypto regulation.
4. **Banking and Prudential Regulation** — CRR3/CRD6, EBA technical standards; PRA Rulebook, Basel 3.1 UK implementation, ring-fencing, IFPR for investment firms.
5. **Markets, Securities and Investment Services** — MiFID II/MiFIR review, Listing Act, EMIR, MAR, Prospectus Regulation; UK Wholesale Markets Review, FCA Conduct of Business sourcebook, UK Listing Rules.
6. **Funds and Asset Management** — AIFMD II, UCITS, ELTIF 2.0; UK AIFM Regulations, FCA COLL.
7. **Consumer Credit, Lending and Mortgages** — EU Consumer Credit Directive (Directive (EU) 2023/2225), Mortgage Credit Directive; UK Consumer Credit Act 1974 reform, FCA CONC and MCOB.
8. **Data Protection and Privacy** — EU GDPR, ePrivacy Directive, EDPB guidelines; UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, Data (Use and Access) Act, ICO guidance.
9. **Open Finance** — FCA Open Finance Roadmap, Open Banking transition and the Future Entity; Smart Data framework and EU FIDA read-across (UK focus).
10. **Cybersecurity and Operational Resilience** — DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554), NIS2; UK FCA/PRA operational resilience (PS21/3, SS1/21, SS2/21), third-party risk regime under FSMA 2023.
11. **Financial Crime, AML and CTF** — EU AML Package (AMLR, AMLD6, AMLA), Transfer of Funds Regulation; UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017, POCA 2002, JMLSG guidance, FATF recommendations as commentary.
12. **Sanctions and Export Controls** — EU restrictive measures regime; UK OFSI guidance, Sanctions and Anti-Money Laundering Act 2018, Russia/Belarus/Iran regimes; extraterritorial OFAC where relevant.
13. **Consumer Protection and Conduct** — UCPD, Modernisation Directive; UK FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 12, PS22/9), Consumer Rights Act 2015.
14. **Competition and Digital Markets** — EU DMA, DSA, antitrust enforcement; UK Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, CMA digital markets unit.
15. **ESG, Sustainability and Climate Disclosure** — SFDR, EU Taxonomy, CSRD/ESRS, CSDDD; UK SDR (PS23/16), TCFD/ISSB, transition plan disclosures.
16. **Corporate Governance and Senior Accountability** — UK SMCR, FCA/PRA individual accountability; UK Corporate Governance Code; DORA governance overlap.
17. **Tax and Regulatory Reporting** — DAC8 (crypto reporting), ATAD, CARF/CRS updates; UK HMRC fintech-specific guidance.
18. **Employment** — Working time, whistleblowing (PIDA, EU Whistleblowing Directive), platform work where relevant.
19. **Intellectual Property** — Trade marks, copyright (AI training data disputes), trade secrets.
20. **Cross-cutting and Horizontal** — Use only when a development genuinely spans three or more topics and cannot be primarily classified. Avoid as a default.
